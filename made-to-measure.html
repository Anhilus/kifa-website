The five steps

Four to six weeks · three fittings · one cutter

    Before you come in

    Bring three
    things.

    Nothing else is needed. No appointment fee, no deposit until the cloth is cut.

    1. The date

      The wedding date, not the shopping date. If there is not enough time for three fittings we will say so at the counter, not in week five.

    2. A garment that fits you

      Any kurta or jacket you already like the fit of. We measure it flat and compare. It settles arguments about the sleeve faster than words.

    3. The shoes

      The shoes you will actually wear. The hem is cut to them. A two-centimetre heel changes where the sherwani breaks.

    Honest answers

    Asked
    and
    answered.

    Can it be done faster than four weeks?
    Sometimes, for a kurta or a waistcoat. Not for a wedding sherwani. We would rather lose the order than hand over a garment that has had one fitting.
    Why is bespoke priced on request?
    Because the cloth decides it. A raw silk sherwani and a zari brocade sherwani are the same labour and very different bills. You are told the number before the shears go in, in writing.
    What if I lose weight before the wedding?
    Tell us at the first measure. We leave inlay in the seams for exactly this and correct it at the last fitting. It is expected, not a problem.
    Do you alter it later?
    Yes. Your measurements stay on file. Bring the garment back whenever the body changes — the first alteration on a KIFA garment is not charged.
