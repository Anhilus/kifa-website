Readymade carries a price on the tag. Bespoke is on request, because the cloth and the hours decide it. Every garment is shown on the ground its colour needs — ivory on indigo, navy on chalk, pastels on iron.

Prices shown are for readymade pieces off the rail and include no alteration charge. Bespoke is quoted in writing before the cloth is cut.

Naming

One name
word, one
garment.

Every piece is a name and a garment type, and nothing else. Noor Sherwani. Riwaaz Kurta. Mehr Bandhgala. Short, rooted, easy to say aloud, and never a number. If a name needs explaining, it does not go on a label.
