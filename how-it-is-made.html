    Honest answers

    Asked
    and
    answered.

    Will you cut one to my measurements?
    No. KIFA sells finished garments only. Everything is cut to our own block, made upstairs, and sold off the rail in sizes 38 to 48. What we will do is alter what you buy until it sits.
    Can I buy the cloth on its own?
    No. We do not sell cloth by the metre and never have. The bolts in the fabric library are what the workshop cuts from, listed so you know exactly what a garment is made of before you buy it.
    What if I lose weight before the wedding?
    Bring it back. We leave inlay in the seams for exactly this, and taking a waist in is forty-eight hours of work, not a new garment. It is expected, not a problem.
    Do you alter it later?
    Yes, for as long as you own it. Bring the garment back whenever the body changes — alterations on a KIFA garment are not charged, and they are done by the workshop that cut it.
