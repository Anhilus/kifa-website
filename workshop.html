The workshop

Forty years, one street, one workshop.

KIFA cuts wedding and occasion menswear from its own cloth, in its own workshop. That last part is the whole business. The cutting table has not moved since 1984 and neither has the shop.

We are not a design house and not a marketplace. We are a running tailoring business with fabric it cuts to order and readymade pieces it sells off the rail — and a reputation built one street at a time, which this page exists to make legible to anyone who has never walked past the door.

    A note on these pictures

    Every image on this site is drawn, not photographed. The workshop shoot has not happened yet, and we would rather show a line drawing we made than a stock photograph of somebody else’s tailor. When the shoot is done, the drawings come down and the real garments go up — in the same frames, on the same grounds.
